The Process

Our proposition is – increase the media offerings to your clients without having to make any actual investments. All you have to do is share the client brief with us, EP shall in turn plan and execute the same on your behalf.

Step-1

You share in your client brief in EP’s campaign brief format, an EP executive will shortly get in touch with your agency to discuss the brief in detail.

Step-2

Once the brief is understood, a customized plan shall be shared and details of the plan will be discussed via a tri party meeting which shall comprise of EP, your agency and the client.

Step-3

Once the campaign is locked, the execution process will start and POE will be shared with you.
