What Is IPL Advertising, and Why Should You Consider Doing IPL Ads?

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is India’s biggest sports league and the world's largest cricket tournament. For brands, IPL advertising is an opportunity to reach millions of unique viewership at once for nine consecutive weeks.

In 2026, IPL Ads offers a range of modes and methods to engage with your audience. It helps your brand with:

  • Massive Reach & Engagement: Tata IPL draws 600M+ viewership, offering brands unparalleled visibility along with nationwide brand building.
  • TV and Digital Dominance: Along with linear TV penetration across rural India, IPL strongly dominates digital and OTT platforms through JioHotstar and Connected TVs (CTVs).
  • Diverse Ad Formats: Brands can engage audiences through pre-roll and mid-roll video ads, branded cards, banner ads, jersey sponsorships, and team associations.
  • For Every Budget: IPL advertising supports brands of all sizes and industries, enabling optimized campaigns aligned with specific marketing objectives.

Types of IPL Advertisements

Video ads have been the most powerful and premium ad format of IPL. Brands use video-based IPL ads during pre-roll, mid-roll, and customised campaigns during times when audiences are hooked to their devices.

IPL banner advertising works best for brands aiming for sustained digital presence throughout the TATA IPL 2026 season. These static or animated ads are budget-friendly and are displayed on live streaming platforms, cricket apps, and sports websites. Also, in the JioHotstar, alongside the streaming of the IPL match.

Brands can opt to sponsor a jersey spot, via which they get special access to players and image rights to use on their product and marketing activities. This partnership goes beyond just the IPL season, where they can use players' images, signs, and more until the next IPL Auction.

Tata IPL is a global event in sports. Covered by every leading sports media, read by all the enthusiasts. This offers premium sponsorship and ad placement opportunities for brands with different budgets and ad formats.

Radio-based IPL Ads reach commuters and regional audiences through live match updates. This format is ideal for local and regional brand visibility. As not every fan can watch every match, they tend to keep a check on scores and highlights through their favourite radio channels of the region.

Newspaper IPL section advertising includes display ads, match-day sponsorships, and branded content in sports pages. This traditional IPL advertising format is effective for brands targeting mature and regional audiences.
