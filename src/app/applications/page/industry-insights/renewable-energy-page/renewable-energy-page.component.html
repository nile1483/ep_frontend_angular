Ad Spends Trend for Renewable Energy Sector

Jan–Dec: 2022, 2023, 2024

In Jan–Dec 2024, the renewable energy sector’s ad spends witnessed a 68% increase on digital, 42% growth in print, and 55% spike in transit media compared to 2022. However, TV spends declined by 23%, reflecting a clear shift to more targeted and sustainable media platforms.

Preferred Media for Advertising in Renewable Energy Sector

Jan–Dec: 2022, 2023, 2024

In Jan–Dec 2024, digital dominated ad spends with a 62% share, driven by rising awareness campaigns and green tech innovations. Transit media held strong with 21% share, especially in urban areas. Print media maintained relevance with 13% share, often used for corporate communication and policy advocacy.
