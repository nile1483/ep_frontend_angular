Jewellery and Accessories Advertising Landscape in September 2026

As per the reports, Digital ad spend in the jewellery industry fell to ₹44.2 crore, down at 19.6% from August and 9.6% compared to September 2025, as the market entered a quieter period between festivals. Despite the overall slowdown, jewellery gained a larger share of advertising, rising to 50.3% of digital spend and 82.2% of TV airtime. With gold prices around 41% higher than a year earlier, Tanishq promoted its Gold Exchange programme on TV. Jewellers also contributed 10% of all radio ad seconds, with Madhya Pradesh leading regional activity. Meanwhile, the advertiser base fell 12.2% to 1,797, and luggage advertising saw a sharp decline in digital spend, dropping from 19% to just 2.1%.
