Digital Advertising Landscape in July 2026

Digital advertising stayed largely stable in July 2026, but AI saw a major jump, rising to 10.98% of digital ad spend from just 0.57% a year earlier. ChatGPT became the most advertised brand, while Adobe Software India led advertisers. The digital market remained highly fragmented, with more advertisers entering but spending smaller amounts individually. Facebook display ads continued to dominate, accounting for 54.8% of attributed spend.
