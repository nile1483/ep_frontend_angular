Radio advertising in the year 2025 continued its upward momentum.

Radio maintained its upward trajectory until the year 2025. The growth pattern developed gradually over three years because advertisement volume increased by 17% during the first year, and 22% during the second year, and 23% during the third year, when compared to the 2022 figure. The medium remained a reliable advertising choice for brands because more advertisers entered the market and multiple advertising categories continued to operate. The system operates without showy elements yet maintains dependable performance, which enabled the radio to remain relevant.
