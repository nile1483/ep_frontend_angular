Print Advertising Saw a Positive Shift

(2025)

During the entire year of 2025, print media experienced continuous growth, which started from its previous year's success. The medium achieved a 19% growth in the advertising industry during 2024, which was followed by an even greater 27% increase in 2025 when compared to the advertising space of 2022.

Leading States in Print Advertising

(2025)

The print advertising market in 2025 showed a predominant regional bias because only a few states generated most of their advertising growth. Rajasthan emerged as the leading market, capturing 15.1% of total print ad space during Jan-Dec 2025 and set the pace for other states. The top five states together contributed 52.9% of all print ad volumes because advertisers used high-readership regions to their advantage.
