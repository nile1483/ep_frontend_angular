Media Insights Based on 2019-2025 Media Trends

Indian Media Survey 2025 (IMS) is a data-driven report that decodes how India's 1.4 billion population has consumed media across TV, Digital, Print, and Radio between 2019 and 2025.

Designed for modern marketers, the report highlights changing audience behavior, evolving media consumption patterns, and the shift in advertising investments across channels. It helps identify the right platforms, right audiences, and right moments to build smarter, more effective brand strategies.

Digital Boom is Reshaping the Indian Media Industry

India reached its fastest digital growth period since 2019, when its internet user base increased from 384 million to over 900 million by 2025. Digital media now serves as the basic foundation for all segments of the Indian media market.

National Media Reach: 2019 vs. 2025

Monthly reach (% of population 12+) shows a dramatic digital surge while traditional media holds ground or contracts.

Internet reach nearly doubled and OTT tripled, while print declined and TV plateaued. The average Indian now accesses ~2.5 media types monthly, up from ~2 in 2019 - reflecting a multi-platform consumption era.

The report provides comprehensive information about the ways digital expansion changes consumer behavior, alters media viewing habits, and develops new advertising methods for different fields of business.

India's Advertising Mix Is Rapidly Realigning

The entire Indian advertising marketplace is expected to grow by an estimated ₹1.37 lakh crore by 2025. Most of this increase has been attributed to the acceptance of digital platforms (particularly mobile).

At the same time:

  • Television continues to deliver strong mass reach, covering around 72% of the population.
  • Print media still connects with approximately 340 million readers every month.
  • Radio maintains a stable presence with about 20% national reach.

Inside the report, you’ll see how these evolving media dynamics are directly influencing advertising investments across industries.
