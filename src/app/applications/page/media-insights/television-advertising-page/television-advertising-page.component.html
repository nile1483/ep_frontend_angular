TV Advertising Landscape in July 2026

TV advertising witnessed a sharp decline in July 2026, with monitored airtime falling 18.3% compared to July 2025, losing around 12,930 hours of monitored airtime in a single month. Star India remained the leading advertiser, contributing 42.2% of estimated TV ad spend while sports channels stood out by generating 63.4% of estimated ad value despite accounting for only 8.5% of airtime, highlighting the premium attached to sports inventory. Broadcaster promos made up 39.1% of total TV airtime, making media the largest super-category at 40% by volume.
