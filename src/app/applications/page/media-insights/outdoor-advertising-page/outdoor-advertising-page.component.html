Outdoor Ad Volume Trends

Jan-Nov: 2022, 2023, 2024

Outdoor advertising witnessed a strong 30% growth in 2023, driven by increased brand visibility efforts post-pandemic. However, in 2024, the market saw stabilization, with a 5% decline compared to the previous year. Real Estate remained the top spending category, accounting for 23.24% of the total outdoor ad share, followed by Consumer Services and Retail. Billboards continued to dominate the medium, attracting the highest number of advertisers.

Outdoor Advertising by Category

Jan-Nov 2024

The Real Estate, Consumer Services, and Retail industries were the leading sectors investing in outdoor advertising, leveraging billboards, transit media, and digital screens to capture consumer attention. Metro cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore led in ad spend, while regional markets saw a rise in transit advertising, signaling a shift toward hyperlocal targeting.
