  • Week-on-Week Formula

One of the most critical indicators for measuring short-term increases in sales, revenue, or other KPIs is the week-on-week growth rate. This formula can be utilized to keep track of business performance, identify trends, and make decisions accordingly.

Week-on-week Formula to measure growth rate:

WOW Growth Rate(%)=
(Current Week Value−Previous Week Value) / Previous Week Value×100

Example:
If x company’s last week's revenue was 100 and this week’s is 120, the growth rate for the week will be:

= 120-100 / 100 ×100
= 20%
