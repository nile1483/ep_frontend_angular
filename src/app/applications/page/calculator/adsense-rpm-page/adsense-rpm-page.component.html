Adsense RPM

Calculator

Measure your website’s revenue potential with our AdSense RPM calculator. You will get the accurate estimation of your revenue generation per thousand impressions to help you in optimizing your monetization strategy. Apply this now!

This AdSense RPM calculator lets you calculate the revenue generation of popular AdSense platforms like Google and Yahoo. Revenue Per Thousand Impressions (RPM) measures how much ad revenue you earn for every thousand impressions on your website. Our calculator makes it easy to estimate your website's ad revenue potential by quickly calculating RPM, helping you maximize your earnings.

  • What is an AdSense RPM calculator?

An AdSense RPM calculator is a tool that helps you estimate how much revenue you can earn from Google AdSense for every 1,000 ad impressions on your website. RPM (Revenue Per Thousand Impressions) is calculated using the formula:

RPM = Estimated earnings*100/ Number of impressions

By inputting your estimated earnings and impressions, the calculator gives you a clear idea of your potential ad revenue, helping you optimize your ad performance and maximize profits.

  • How is AdSense RPM calculated?

AdSense RPM (Revenue Per Thousand Impressions) shows how much money you earn for every 1,000 times ads are shown on your website. A higher RPM means your ads are performing well and generating more income. It helps you understand how effectively your website is monetizing traffic, allowing you to adjust your ad placements and content strategy to maximize revenue.

Example:

If your estimated earnings are ₹500 from 10,000 impressions, the RPM would be:

500*100/1000 =50

This means you’re earning ₹50 for every 1,000 ad impressions on your website.

  • How much does AdSense pay per 1000 views?

The amount AdSense pays per 1,000 views (RPM) can vary depending on factors like your website's niche, audience location, ad quality, and user engagement. On average, AdSense pays between ₹100 to ₹600 per 1,000 views for Indian traffic, while for US or European traffic, it can range from $1 to $10 or even higher. For example, if your website earns ₹300 for 10,000 views, your RPM would be ₹30 (₹300 ÷ 10 = ₹30 per 1,000 views). High-paying niches like finance, technology, and health tend to have higher RPMs, whereas entertainment or general content may have lower rates.

  • Factors affecting AdSense earnings
  • Niche/Industry – High-paying niches like finance, technology, and health tend to have higher RPMs compared to entertainment or general content.
  • Traffic Source – Traffic from countries like the US, UK, and Canada generally results in higher payouts than traffic from developing countries.
  • Ad Placement – Strategically placing ads where they are more likely to be seen and clicked (like above the fold or within content) increases revenue.
  • Ad Type and Format – Display ads, video, and native ads have different payout rates, with video ads usually paying more.
  • Audience Engagement – Higher click-through rates (CTR) and longer time spent on-site increase the chances of higher earnings.
  • Device Type – Earnings can vary depending on whether the traffic comes from desktop, mobile, or tablet, as desktop traffic often generates higher revenue.
  • Ad Blockers – If users have ad blockers enabled, your ads won’t be shown, reducing your potential earnings.
