This is how you can list your media

Listing your media on our platform is a hassle-free process that can be completed in a few simple steps:

Reach out to our dedicated team

Contact us using the provided contact number to express your interest in listing your media on Excellent Publicity's platform.

Form submission

Our team will guide you through the process of filling out a form. This form will require essential information such as your name, contact number, official email ID, and the name of your company.

Data and image upload

Along with the form, you will be prompted to upload the necessary data and images related to your media. This could include creative assets, descriptions, or any other relevant information.

Seamless evaluation

Once we receive your submission, our expert team will carefully evaluate your media to ensure it meets our quality standards and aligns with our platform's guidelines.

Swift upload process

After the evaluation process, we will initiate the upload of your media on our platform. Within just one week, your media will be live and ready to captivate a diverse and engaged audience.
